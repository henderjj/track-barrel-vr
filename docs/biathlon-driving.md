# Biathlon: the driving challenge (F5.3d)

John's note after playing the biathlon (2026-11-13): the driving had no skill in it, since the tank could go flat out all the way round. He picked five changes (options 1 to 5) and said the 4° tilt cap shouldn't hold the tank's movement back, since the view from inside is so restricted. The shooting side (F5.3c) is in [biathlon-race.md](biathlon-race.md#harder-shooting). Rolling the tank over (option 6) is not wanted.

The result: a lap can't be driven flat out. The driver picks a gear for the hill, slows into the tight corners, picks a line through the slalom and onto the bridge, eases off in the mud or takes the bypass, and crawls through the ford.

## What the players meet

A lap of the 817 m loop, in the order it meets them (the course is in [biathlon-course.md](biathlon-course.md); all numbers are in `src/sim/course.ts`, `src/sim/terrain.ts` and `src/sim/going.ts`):

1. **The slalom** (north side). Four gates of two red-and-white posts, 4.4 m apart (a Sherman is 2.6 m wide). Each gate is set 2 m to one side of the middle line, then the other side, 16 m apart. A post the hull touches falls over, and a gate passed outside its posts counts as missed. Each one is a **fault** that adds 10 s to a race's time, and the course's sign says so ("Slalom: post knocked down, +10 s"). Knocked posts stand up again at the next lap.
2. **The hill** (option 1), straight after the slalom: a ridge 7 m high and 96 m long across the track. Its steepest middle part is about 12°, which 2nd gear climbs at full throttle and 3rd does not. The automatic box changes down by itself, and it only changes up again once the next gear can carry the load. With the stick, a driver in too high a gear slows, stops and rolls back, or stalls. Down the far side the tank runs on faster than its gear's flat top speed (the engine holds back 60% of its gear's pull, the brakes the rest), straight into the tight north-west corner.
3. **Tight corners** (option 3): the two west corners are 20 m in radius (the east ones stay 30 m). Turning pushes the tracks sideways by speed × turn rate, plus some lean on a slope, and 35% more on mud. Past 2.0 m/s² the driver's sign says "Tracks straining", and the tracks are heard straining well before that (docs/audio.md). Past 2.3 m/s² for about a second, the outside track comes off. Both players see "Right track thrown", and the tank is held while the crew refit it for 40 s. At speed the turn rate tops out at 15°/s, so a full turn on firm ground pushes the tracks by the speed × 0.26: the warning comes from about 27 km/h and a thrown track from about 32 km/h, or about 24 km/h on mud. Going that fast the tank can't turn tighter than about 34 m, so the tight corners are taken slower or run wide. (The limits were 1.5 and 1.9 m/s² until John's play test of 2026-11-14: then any turn above about 21 km/h warned, and a hard one above 26 km/h threw a track.)
4. **The treadway bridge** (option 5), on the west side over a ditch 2.5 m deep with water in its bottom. It has two steel treads, 1 m wide, under the tracks, 38 m long with ramps, standing 25 cm proud. A track off its tread over the ditch drops the tank in: a **fall** ([below](#a-fall-off-the-bridge)). Gate 4 just past the bridge makes the bridge part of every lap.
5. **The mud** (option 2), on the south side, 24 m long and 18 m wide, darker brown. It drags at the tracks: 3rd gear carries it (about 14 km/h) and 4th does not. More than 60% throttle spins the tracks at any speed, and the tank digs in ("Digging in") until it is **bogged** after about 3 s at full throttle ("Bogged in the mud: reverse out, then take the bypass"). Bogged, it can only back out. The mud slows a tank driven into it up to twice as hard as the comfort limit on acceleration, so a tank can't coast across on its momentum. A firm gravel **bypass** goes round the mud as a **walled lane** (John, 17 November: the S-bends could be straight-lined). It turns hard left off the course into the infield, runs 56 m out, turns right along and right again back, and turns left onto the course. Its four corners are right angles between dry stone walls 1.5 m high, 9 m apart, so a corner can't be cut, and a driver who takes one too fast throws a track or stops against the wall. Two walls across the field at the mud's middle close the grass round it: one from the mud north past the bypass to 120 m into the infield, one south from the mud to a 160 m wall along the field short of the range's cross road. So a tank goes through the mud, round the walled lane, or a long way round a wall's end.

Driven well, the mud is the quicker way (John, 14 November). From 10 m before the bypass leaves to 10 m past where it joins, easing through the mud at 60% throttle takes 20.6 s in the drive model. The quickest line round the walled lane takes 24.7 s, found by shaking a line between the walls until it can't get quicker, for a driver with perfect throttle who takes every corner at the limit before a track comes off (`test/support/fastest-line.ts`, checked in `test/going.test.ts`). Round either wall's end is at least 14 s slower than the course, even in straight lines at full speed. Floored into the mud, the tank bogs and loses far more.
6. **The ford** (option 5), a stream 0.9 m deep across the south side. Faster than 8 km/h through water deeper than 25 cm for a second, the bow wave drowns the engine ("Drowned in the ford: restart after 12 s, ford slower"), and the starter won't turn it for 12 s.

### A fall off the bridge

John's play test of 2026-11-15: the jump back to the bridge's start was abrupt, there should be a clear time penalty, and the tank should be off and need restarting. Now, in the order it happens (numbers in `BRIDGE_FALL`, `FALL_SECONDS` in `src/sim/course.ts`):

1. A track goes off its tread over the ditch. The engine dies at once, and the tracks are held as the parking brake holds them, so the tank slows to a stop (no faster than the comfort limit) and tips with the ditch's edge as it goes.
2. The whole view fades to black over 0.8 s, for both players, using the same black that fades the view when a head leans into the armour.
3. It stays black for 0.6 s. Halfway through, the tank goes back to the bridge's start, 8 m short of the ramp, lined up and at rest. (Halfway, so the frame that moves it is already black: the fade a frame shows is the one worked out the frame before.)
4. The view fades back in over 0.9 s. Until it has, nothing drives the tank and the starter does nothing.
5. The engine is off. The driver's sign says "Engine stalled. Off the bridge: +30 s. Start it again", and the driver starts it as after any stall (the starter button, or the Full procedure). This holds with Engine start: Running too, which only starts the engine when it is chosen. The practice crewmate restarts it itself.
6. The course's sign says "Fell off the bridge: +30 s. Restart the engine". In a race, each fall adds **30 s** to the time, on top of the time lost restarting and lining up again. The race's clocks show the penalties earned so far on the top line ("Lap 2/3   +0:30", slalom faults included), and the finish counts the falls ("Falls 1" on the clocks, "1 bridge fall" in the menu).

The tank's packet carries the fall's time still to go (one more byte; a page on the previous build reads no fall), so the guest's view fades with the host's. The race's `run` event gains `falls`. A tank a Practice shortcut places mid-fall is placed with no fall.

**Heat from load** (option 4): working against a slope or mud at more than 40% of what the gear can push heats the coolant like F3.7's high revs. At the full load the gear can push, it heads for 80% of F3.7's top strain heat. A long climb in too high a gear, or churning in the mud, brings the TEMP lamp on.

**The tilt** (John's 13 November decision): the hull's pitch and roll limit went from 4° to 15°, so the tank tips with the ground on the hill, the ditch banks and the ford. Reduced bumps (Comfort) still cap it at 6°.

**Signs** (from John's play test of 2026-11-15, [biathlon-signs.md](biathlon-signs.md)): a SLALOM board and red and blue gates as in ski slalom, a caution triangle with MUD before the bypass leaves, an 8 km/h limit roundel with FORD before the water, and PENALTY LOOP with chevrons round the loop.

## How it works

- **Engine-free rules** in `src/sim/going.ts`: each gear's steepest climb (`GEAR_GRADE`: 1st 25°, 2nd 15°, 3rd 9°, 4th 5.5°, 5th 3°, reverse 15°), `groundPull`, which leaves over whatever slope and drag the engine and brakes can't answer, the mud's sink, the throw strain and the load's heat share. `src/sim/tank-drive.ts` applies them each step. Its flat-ground drive is unchanged, the ground's leftover pull is added under the same comfort limit on acceleration, and `stepGoing` does the sink, the throw, the ford and the bridge fall.
- **The ground's shape** is in `src/sim/terrain.ts` (`courseRelief`: the hill, the ditch and the stream as part of the height field). The tracks follow it, and the drawn gravel adds a point wherever its middle line crosses a cell line of the height field, so it lies on the ground over the crest and through the ford.
- **Host-authoritative.** The host's drive sim decides everything. The tank packet (`PacketType` 5) grows by 4 bytes: sink, sideways push, which track is thrown and the refit's seconds left (and since the fall's fade a fifth, the fall's time to go). A page on the previous build still decodes the old length (no going). The course's `course` event gains faults, knocked posts, slalom gates and falls. The race's `run` event gains faults (and falls). No new packet types or HoldKinds.
- **Surfaces**: mud, water and steel join grass, dirt and gravel for the track sounds (`src/sim/ground-surface.ts`).
- **The crewmate** drives the course at a speed set for each stretch of the course: 9 km/h through the slalom (weaving through the gates), 16 down the hill and round the tight corners, 8 over the bridge, 12 through the mud (its tillers well under the 60% that digs a tank in) and 7 through the ford. It brakes when it runs on downhill. A clean race takes it 13:12, and three penalty loops take it 15:32.
- **Medal times** retuned from that: gold 12:30, silver 13:30, bronze 15:45.

## Practice

Game menu → **Practice → Obstacles**: Slalom, Hill, Bridge, Mud or Ford parks the tank on the course short of that obstacle, facing it the way a lap drives it, with the engine running. Alone, you take the driver's seat. In a crew, the seats stay as they are.

Test hooks: `window.__course.layout()` gives `posts`, `bridge` and `obstacles`, `__course.posts()` gives each post as drawn and whether it is down, `__course.obstacle(name)` runs the shortcut, and `__drive.going({ sink, thrown, refit })` sets the going on the host. XR step 6x is in part 4: it also checks that a fall fades the view to black, moves the tank while it is black, clears again and leaves the engine off. The net test checks that a thrown track reaches the guest, and that a fall on the host fades the guest's view and gives it the +30 s.

## Headset check

On a Quest 3 after the deploy, with the cache cleared (Ctrl+Shift+R), seated, in the driver's seat.

1. **Practice → Obstacles → Slalom**. Drive through the four gates. Say whether you can see the posts from the slit or the periscope in time to steer, whether a knocked post is clear (it lies down and the sign says "+10 s"), and whether the gates are too tight or too easy.
2. **Obstacles → Hill**. Full ahead with the automatic box: it should change down to 2nd near the top and keep climbing. Then try the stick in 4th or 5th: it should slow and stall. Over the crest, say how the run-on downhill feels, and whether the 15° tilt is comfortable or too much.
3. Take the tight north-west corner fast (over 30 km/h): the tracks groan and squeak, "Tracks straining", then "Right track thrown" and a 40 s wait. Say whether the warning comes early enough to save it, and whether a turn at 20 to 25 km/h now goes without a warning.
4. **Obstacles → Bridge**. Line up and cross slowly. Say whether you can see the treads well enough to stay on them. Then drive off on purpose: the engine should cut, the view fade to black and back in about two seconds, and the tank be at the bridge's start with the engine off, the signs saying +30 s and to restart it. Say whether the fade is smooth (no glimpse of the jump, no lurch), whether tipping towards the ditch as it goes dark is uncomfortable, and whether 30 s feels right. Then start the engine and cross. In a crew, the other player should see the same fade.
5. **Obstacles → Mud**. Full throttle into the mud: "Digging in", then "Bogged". Reverse out and take the walled bypass as fast as you dare: try to cut its corners. Then try easing through the mud at just over half throttle in 3rd. The mud should be the quicker way. Say whether it is, whether you found any way to cut the bypass or get round the walls, whether the walls are easy to see from the driver's seat, and whether the mud's slowing as you drive in feels too sharp.
6. **Obstacles → Ford**. Cross at a crawl (under 8 km/h on the speedometer), then again fast: the engine drowns with the sign, and the starter won't turn it for 12 s.
7. Drive a whole race (**Biathlon race → Start**) with the crewmate as gunner. Say your time and medal (gold 12:30, silver 13:30, bronze 15:45), whether the TEMP lamp came on, and how many faults you got.
8. Watch the crewmate drive a race from the gunner's seat. Say whether it looks careful but not silly.
9. With the performance HUD on round the course, say whether the frame time stays as before.
